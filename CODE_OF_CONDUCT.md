# Code of Conduct

Kael adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Community members must be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Examples of positive participation include giving actionable feedback, accepting
responsibility, respecting different experiences, and focusing on what is best
for the community.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces represented as part of the Kael project.

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainers through the relevant
GitHub thread. For a private report, use the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/Th4phat/kael-agent/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct:`.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the
safety of affected community members.

Instances of abusive or illegal behavior may also be reported through GitHub's
platform abuse-reporting mechanisms.
